This protocol is written before subject execution. This is a migration/operational comparison by one informed assistant, not a blind discovery study or a human productivity experiment.

## Outcome and accounting

An obtained counterexample needs explicit requirement, executable scenario, a failing observed count/status, and a replay command. A paired counterexample additionally needs the SAME scenario executed on both versions, with both observations preserved. A source frame identifies the callback invocation, not necessarily the root cause.

Record per-project API/contract fields, source/runtime preparation, specialized executable test lines, process/command attempts, replay agreement, inconclusive results and raw traces. Shared download/preparation and common framework/model implementation costs must be disclosed. Lines/fields are artifact inventory, not mental effort or developer minutes. Execution times are mechanical process durations only; coverage and isolation differ, so do not rank developer efficiency from them.

Report P/F comparable domain adaptation separately from N's handwritten witness. Freeze all implementations before observing outputs; save source hashes before and after. Include the fixed control and all residual failures. Do not add templates or repair unsupported cases during this comparison.

Stop after these three arms on this selected pair. The research question is whether the workflow saves observable adaptation work, not whether another known bug can be demonstrated. A genuine productivity claim would additionally require fresh users or controlled independent sessions and more independently selected changes.
