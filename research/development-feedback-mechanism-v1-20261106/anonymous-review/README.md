# Review package

Candidate IDs and order are random. The private condition/unit/generation-order map is outside this package. Use evidence/<alias>/key.json, raw evidence and parent.md, and candidates/<ID>/skill.md/notes.md/parent.diff.

Labels alone are hidden; content, parent skills and necessary raw evidence can reveal source or suggest condition. The executing agent participated in preparation: its review MUST be marked unblinded proxy, not independent human or independent blind assessment. No LLM judge is invoked. Keep a blank form for future independent human review. Freeze review.csv before unblinding.

Eight candidates are two units with two repetitions per condition, not eight independent tasks.
